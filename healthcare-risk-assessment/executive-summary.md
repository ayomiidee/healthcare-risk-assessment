# Executive Summary

**Client:** Maple Grove Family Clinic (fictional)
**Prepared by:** Ayomide Oriowo
**Scope:** Information security risk across IT systems, data, and staff practices

## Bottom Line

The clinic holds sensitive patient data but has basic security gaps. Four risks rate High. All four can be reduced with low cost fixes in the next 90 days.

## Top Risks

| Rank | Risk | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remote access without multi factor authentication | 20 |
| 2 | Phishing leads to stolen credentials | 16 |
| 3 | Unpatched systems | 16 |
| 4 | Ransomware on the EHR server | 15 |
| 5 | No security awareness training | 15 |

## What We Recommend

**First 30 days**
- Turn on MFA for email, EHR, and remote access.
- Start monthly patching with a simple checklist.

**30 to 60 days**
- Run security awareness training for all staff.
- Test restoring from backup, and keep one copy offline.

**60 to 90 days**
- Approve and publish the access control and incident response policies.
- Review vendor security for the billing provider.

## Why It Matters

A breach of patient data can trigger HIPAA fines, lawsuits, and loss of patient trust. A ransomware attack could also stop patient care. The fixes above cost far less than a single incident.
