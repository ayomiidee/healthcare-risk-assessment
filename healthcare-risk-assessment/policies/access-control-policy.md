# Access Control Policy

**Organization:** Maple Grove Family Clinic (fictional)
**Owner:** Clinic Manager
**Version:** 1.0
**Review:** Every year
**Related controls:** NIST 800-53 AC-2, AC-6, IA-2

## 1. Purpose
Protect patient and business data by making sure only the right people can access it.

## 2. Scope
All staff, contractors, and vendors who use clinic systems or data.

## 3. Policy

### 3.1 Least Privilege
Staff get only the access needed for their job. Access is based on role.

### 3.2 Account Management
- Every user has a unique account. No shared accounts.
- Managers request access in writing.
- Accounts are disabled on the employee's last day.
- Access is reviewed every quarter.

### 3.3 Authentication
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
- MFA is required for email, the EHR, and all remote access.
- Passwords must not be reused across systems.

### 3.4 Privileged Access
- Admin accounts are separate from daily accounts.
- Only the IT contractor holds admin rights.
- Admin activity is logged.

### 3.5 Patient Record Access
- Staff may open a patient record only for treatment, payment, or operations.
- Access to records is logged and reviewed.

## 4. Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Clinic Manager | Approves access and runs reviews |
| IT Contractor | Sets up, removes, and monitors accounts |
| All Staff | Protect credentials and report misuse |

## 5. Enforcement
Breaking this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions need written approval from the Clinic Manager and are reviewed every 6 months.
