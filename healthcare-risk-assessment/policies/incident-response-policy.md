# Incident Response Policy

**Organization:** Maple Grove Family Clinic (fictional)
**Owner:** Security Lead
**Version:** 1.0
**Review:** Every year
**Related controls:** NIST 800-53 IR-4, IR-6, IR-8

## 1. Purpose
Make sure the clinic responds quickly and clearly to security incidents.

## 2. What Is an Incident
Any event that may harm the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of clinic data or systems. Examples: phishing click, lost laptop, malware, unauthorized record access, ransomware.

## 3. Reporting
- Staff must report suspected incidents right away.
- Report to the IT Contractor and the Clinic Manager.
- Do not delete evidence or turn off devices unless told to.

## 4. Severity Levels
| Level | Example | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Single spam email | 1 business day |
| Medium | Phishing click, no known loss | 4 hours |
| High | Ransomware or confirmed data exposure | Immediate |

## 5. Response Phases
1. **Preparation:** Keep contacts, backups, and this plan current.
2. **Detection and Analysis:** Confirm the incident and set severity.
3. **Containment:** Disconnect affected devices. Disable affected accounts.
4. **Eradication:** Remove malware and fix the cause.
5. **Recovery:** Restore from clean backups and watch for repeat activity.
6. **Lessons Learned:** Hold a review within 2 weeks. Update this plan.

## 6. Roles
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Lead | Runs the response and makes decisions |
| IT Contractor | Technical containment and recovery |
| Clinic Manager | Staff updates and patient communication |
| Legal Counsel | Advises on breach notification |

## 7. Breach Notification
If patient data is exposed, legal counsel decides if notice is required under HIPAA and state law. HIPAA generally requires notice to affected people within 60 days of discovery.

## 8. Testing
Run one tabletop exercise each year and record the results.
